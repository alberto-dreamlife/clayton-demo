CLAYTON
A presale project website, built by Dreamlife


HOW TO OPEN IT

Unzip the folder anywhere, then double click index.html. It opens in your
browser and everything works offline: there is no server to start, no build
step, no dependencies to install. Chrome, Safari and Edge are all fine.

If you want to see it as a phone sees it, open it in Chrome, press Cmd+Option+I
on a Mac or F12 on Windows, and click the small phone icon at the top left of
the panel that appears. Pick any iPhone from the dropdown. That view is worth a
minute of your time, since a good half of the traffic a project like this gets
arrives on a phone.

                    floorplates and every plan

Ten pages, no framework, no content management system. The whole thing is
plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, which is why it opens straight off your disk
and why it loads in well under a second on a real connection.


WHERE TO START LOOKING

The home selector is the piece that matters most, so give it the longest look.
On a computer you pick a building on the aerial, then a level on the render of
the building itself, then a home on the floorplate, and the plan opens beside
it. Nothing you clicked disappears while you do it, so you never lose your place
in the building.

On a phone that same page works a different way, because hunting for a small
shape on a drawing needs a cursor. It leads with the question a buyer actually
arrives with, which is how many bedrooms, then shows the floorplate underneath
with those homes shaded so you can see where they sit, then the plans
themselves. Same data, asked in the order a thumb can handle.

The 360 tour has a plan in the corner that follows you from room to room. You
can minimise it, bring it back, or open it full screen.
