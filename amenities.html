Amenities

The building is
part of the home.

Some days the second bedroom is an office. Some days the office is downstairs, the gym is two floors up, and dinner is on the rooftop. Everything here is shared by 198 homes and owned by all of them.

Level 2, Quadra

The commute is an elevator.

A proper co-working lounge, not a corner with a desk: booths for calls, a long table for laptops, and the coffee within reach. Book a seat downstairs and keep the kitchen table for dinner.

Ground floor, Quadra

Drop-off, thirty seconds.

A licensed daycare inside the building, with its own patio off the courtyard. The kind of amenity that changes a family's whole morning, which is why it sits at the front door and not in a brochure footnote.

Level 2, Saturna

No membership, no excuse.

Cardio at the glass, weights along the mirror, and room to stretch in between. Open early, open late, and never more than an elevator away from your own shower.

Level 1, Quadra

The biggest room you own.

Pool table, lounge seating and a screen for game night. The living room for the nights your living room is not big enough.

The arrival

First impressions, daily.

Double height glass, warm timber and a place to wait that doesn't feel like waiting. Parcel storage sits off the lobby, because the boxes arrive whether you are home or not.

Rainy days

An indoors worth going to.

A playroom scaled to its audience, soft where it should be and washable where it must be. Twenty steps from the daycare, so the youngest residents own this corner of the building.

The rooftops

Dinner, six storeys up.

Both buildings finish in a shared terrace: dining tables, loungers, planting, and the long view over West Clayton. Quadra's faces the sunset, Saturna's faces the courtyard, and every home has a claim on both.
