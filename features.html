The building keeps its promises too.

What surrounds the home matters as much as what is inside it. This page covers comfort, the shared spaces, and the warranty that stands behind all of it.

Comfort and quiet

  • Individually controlled heating and cooling in every home
  • Energy-recovery ventilation supplying continuous fresh air
  • Acoustic underlay and resilient channel separation between homes
  • Solid-core entry doors with weather-sealed frames
  • LED lighting throughout, roughed-in dimmers in living and dining

Connected

  • Fibre-to-the-home internet, wired to every unit
  • USB-C charging outlets in kitchen and principal bedroom
  • Smart entry system with camera intercom to your phone
  • EV-ready parking stalls; rough-in at every residential stall
  • Secure bike storage with repair stand and wash station

The shared spaces

  • Co-working lounge with call booths, level 2 of Quadra
  • Licensed daycare with courtyard patio at the ground floor
  • Fitness studio, level 2 of Saturna
  • Games room and kids playroom off the courtyard
  • Two furnished rooftop terraces with dining and loungers
  • Landscaped courtyard connecting Quadra and Saturna
  • Parcel storage off the lobby; cold storage for grocery delivery

Peace of mind

  • 2-5-10 National Home Warranty: two years on materials and labour, five on the building envelope, ten on structure
  • Restricted-access parkade with security cameras at entries
  • Fire sprinklers throughout homes and common areas
  • Deposit protection held in trust as required in British Columbia

The numbers

  • 198 homes across two six-storey buildings
  • Studios to three-bedroom homes, 451 to 1,253 sq ft
  • Junior one-bedroom and den plans for first buyers
  • Adaptable homes on every residential level
