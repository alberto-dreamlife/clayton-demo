Look closer

The kitchen,
point by point.

On the point
Samsung, set flush.

A French-door refrigerator in a fingerprint-resistant finish, set flush with the cabinetry so the run of the kitchen stays clean. The range, dishwasher and microwave match it.

Uppers with no handles.

Flat-panel upper cabinets open on a push, so the wall reads as one surface. Soft-close hinges and drawers everywhere below.

Light under the cabinets.

An LED strip under the uppers washes the ribbed tile backsplash, so the counter is lit where you actually work and the pendants can stay low over the island.

An island you can sit at.

Square-edge quartz with room for three stools on the working side, and drawers rather than doors underneath. Most plans have one; the rest get an eating bar.

Wide plank, wall to wall.

Laminate planks laid the length of the room, through the living areas and into the bedrooms, over an acoustic underlay you will never hear your neighbour through.

The living roomNine foot ceilings

Floor-to-ceiling glass with a solar coating, roller shades on every window, and a balcony off the living room in every plan.

The bedroomQuiet by design

The same plank flooring as the living room, USB-C at the bedside, and resilient-channel walls between you and next door.

The ensuiteFollows your scheme

A double vanity in quartz with undermount basins, a walk-in shower, and a lit mirror over the lot. Porcelain to the ceiling.

The main bathDeep soaker tub

Full-height tile at the tub, a quartz vanity, and a dual-flush toilet that uses half the water.

The denFibre to every home

Wired for work: fibre internet to the home, Cat6 at the desk, and a door that closes when the call starts.

The second bedroomRoom to grow

Two and three bedroom plans put the second bedroom on the other side of the living room, with its own bath a step away.
